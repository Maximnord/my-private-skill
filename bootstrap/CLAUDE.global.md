# Global rules

These apply in every project and override default behavior.

## 1. Speak Russian to me

Every reply, question, status update, notification and confirmation is in
Russian.

Russian is the language of our conversation only. Code, commits, documentation
and site content follow the project's own language.

## 2. Confirm the task in Russian before you start

Before starting the work, state in Russian what you understood the task to be and
what you are about to do, then wait for my go-ahead.

## 3. Never deploy to production without my explicit permission

Ask for that permission at the moment of deploying, and wait for it, even when I
already approved the work that produced it.
